# Quests and XP

Using AchSwap earns **XP**. Swaps, bridges, liquidity, a daily GM, tasks and inviting friends all add to one total, which sets your **level** (1 to 20) and your place on the **leaderboard**. Everything happens on the Quests page at [trade.achswap.app/quests](https://trade.achswap.app/quests). Your wallet is your account: connect it and you are in.

There is nothing to claim. Task and GM XP is added the moment you finish. Swaps, bridges, liquidity and referrals are added every day shortly after 00:00 UTC, for the day that just ended. See [when XP is added](/quests/distribution).

## The Quests page

| Page | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | Your profile, level and XP, what the next daily distribution will add, the twenty levels, and your XP history award by award |
| **Activity** | Today's swaps, bridges and liquidity on the daily ladder, and how much each earns |
| **Tasks** | The daily GM and every task, with its reward |
| **Leaderboard** | Your rank, and the top wallets by XP, liquidity or volume |
| **Invite** | Your invite link and what it earns |

| 4 | 45 | 9 | 450 | 14 | 3,100 | 19 | 20,500 |
| 5 | 75 | 10 | 650 | 15 | 4,500 | 20 | 30,000 |

Every wallet starts at level 1. The first levels come within a day; each level after that costs about 1.45 times the one before, so level 20 takes more than a year of the most XP a wallet can earn. When you reach a new level, the page plays a short level-up animation once, on whichever device you open next.

## Your profile

- **Username:** shown on the leaderboard instead of your wallet address. 3 to 20 characters: letters, numbers and underscores. Your first username earns 20 XP, once. You can change it later; a name another wallet uses is taken.
- **Profile picture:** click your avatar on the Overview. PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF, up to 2 MB. It is cropped to a square (a GIF shows its first frame) and shown on your profile and the leaderboard. You can remove it at any time.

Setting a username or a picture asks your wallet to sign a message. Signing costs no gas and moves no funds.

## The leaderboard

Wallets are ranked by XP. The **Liquidity** and **Volume** tabs re-sort the same wallets by their eligible liquidity and their swap and bridge volume. AchSwap's own wallets (the deployer and the gasless relayers) earn no XP and are not listed.
